# Deliverables

## Publishable external draft: `<slug>-publish.md`

Include only reader-facing material:

- title and optional subtitle;
- complete article in the requested format and length;
- optional source note at the end when suitable for publication.

Keep academic caveats proportionate: include the limitations needed to prevent misleading interpretation, but do not turn the draft into a methods appendix.
